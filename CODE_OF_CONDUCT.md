# ABISGPT Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the
ABISGPT community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Scope

This Code applies within all project spaces (repository, issues, PRs, chats,
conferences) and when representing the project publicly.

Because biometrics touches on surveillance, policing, and personal privacy,
we additionally pledge to evaluate technical contributions not only for
correctness but for **rights impact**. Contributions that enable covert
surveillance, non-consensual identification, or profiling of vulnerable
groups may be declined at maintainers' discretion even if technically sound.

## Enforcement

Report abusive behavior to the maintainers via
**conduct@[replace-with-domain]** or a private GitHub security-style contact.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may result
in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.
